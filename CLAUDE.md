# Sizzle Demo

**Goal:** a ~90-second, live, clickable fake product demo that solves a real job pain — something we could show a customer, hire, or investor.

## Rules
- One strong path, no login, realistic fake data. Interactive (click/type/play), not slides.
- Plain HTML/CSS/JS in `app/` (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`, `data.js`). Regular `<script>` tags, data in `.js` not `.json`. Must open by double-clicking `app/index.html` — no server, build, or installs. CDN libraries OK.
- No outside APIs. Simulate the AI with a short progress log and believable results.
- Design: graphics over text, readable across a room in 5 seconds, big type, generous spacing, one accent color. Underbuild.
- Priorities: working demo → 90-second `script.md` → polish.
- Keep `plan.md` updated when decisions change. Interview answers live in `context/brief.md`; research notes in `research/`.
- Don't `git init` at the parent `~/saumyaclaude` folder.
