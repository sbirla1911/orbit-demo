# Brief (interview answers)

1. **Who is watching:** College students, 18–35, undergrad or grad.
   _Assumption:_ the demo persona is a first-year student new to campus who hasn't made friends yet. The audience may be a student, a university dean, or an investor.
2. **Painful job:** Making friends in college is hard. It's all self-driven: you find people, and you either click or you don't.
3. **Number they care about:** How many new friends they make, and how many experiences they share with them.
   _Assumption:_ the headline number is "new friends this semester: ~1 on your own → 6+ with the app."
4. **Point of view:** No formal app solves this today. Our app is for universities. Students enter their interests, details, favorite activities, and budget, and they sync their calendar. The app suggests peer matches (groups of 2 or more). If everyone accepts, it books the activity and handles payment in the app. It then strengthens friendships by scheduling repeat meetups. Students can choose scope: their own university, across universities, or their whole city.
5. **Live input:** Age, interests, activities (plus budget and scope).
6. **Data:** A directory of student names and their interests. We'll use realistic fake data.
