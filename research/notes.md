# Research notes

- Loneliness is widely reported among students and young adults. Cigna's 2018 loneliness survey found Gen Z (18–22) was the loneliest generation. National student health surveys (ACHA-NCHA) regularly show about half of students reporting loneliness. Verify the exact figures before quoting them to an investor.
- What exists today: club fairs, Greek life, generic social apps (Bumble BFF, Meetup), and group chats. None of them combine matching with calendar overlap, budget, and booking in one place, and none are run with the university.
- Demo implication: the "magic" is going from *match* to *booked plan* with zero coordination. Show that moment big.
